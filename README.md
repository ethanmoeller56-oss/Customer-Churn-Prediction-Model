Customer Churn Prediction Using Machine Learning

Predicting customer churn is one of the most valuable applications of machine learning in subscription-based businesses. In this project, I built and compared multiple classification models to identify customers at risk of leaving a telecommunications company and uncover the key factors driving churn. The final solution uses a Tuned Random Forest model, selected based on its strong ability to identify customers likely to churn while maintaining balanced predictive performance.

Business Problem

Customer retention is significantly more cost-effective than customer acquisition. By accurately identifying customers at high risk of churning, organizations can proactively target retention campaigns, improve customer satisfaction, and reduce revenue loss.

The goal of this project was to:

Predict whether a customer will churn.
Identify the factors that contribute most to customer churn.
Compare multiple machine learning algorithms.
Recommend actionable business strategies to improve customer retention.
Dataset

Source: IBM Telco Customer Churn Dataset (Kaggle)

The dataset contains customer demographic information, subscription details, account history, billing information, and whether the customer ultimately churned.

Target Variable

Churn Label
